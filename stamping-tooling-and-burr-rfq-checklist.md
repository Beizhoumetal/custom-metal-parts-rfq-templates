# Metal Stamping Die, Tooling Cost & Burr Acceptance RFQ Checklist

Use this non-confidential checklist before requesting metal stamping quotes so every bidder aligns on **formed part geometry, die concept, tooling lifecycle terms, burr/edge criteria, stainless forming behavior, and First Article Inspection (FAI)** before tooling is released.

> **Start Non-Confidentially:** Share a non-confidential outline first (part family, material grade/thickness/temper, tryout lot and annual demand estimate, critical edges or cosmetic faces, and destination). Confirm confidentiality arrangements, named manufacturing partner recipients, and secure file-transfer channels before sending proprietary 2D drawings or 3D CAD models.

---

## 1. Operation Route, Die Architecture & Acceptance Matrix

| Review Area | What to Clarify Before Quoting | Ambiguity to Prevent | Target Reference Guide |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stamping Operations Route** | Define required stages on the finished part (**drawing, trimming, piercing, flanging, restriking, sizing**) and separate forming operations from delivered dimensional acceptance | Quoting from a generic press tonnage list instead of finished-part operation gates | [Metal Stamping Operations & Route Review](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/metal-stamping-operations/) |
| **Die Concept (Progressive vs. Transfer)** | Compare single-strip **progressive tooling** vs. multi-station **transfer tooling** against part size, deep-draw stability, coil utilization, and volume tiers | Comparing progressive vs. stage/transfer proposals with mismatched material yield or handling scope | [Progressive vs. Transfer Die Stamping](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/progressive-vs-transfer-die-stamping/) |
| **Tooling Cost & Ownership Terms** | Separate one-time **engineering design, die build, tryout samples, and minor tuning/rework**, and specify die ownership, maintenance responsibility, and storage period | Hiding tryout scrap, fixture modifications, or tool amortization inside initial unit rates | [What Affects Metal Stamping Tooling Cost?](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/stamping-tooling-cost/) |
| **Blanking vs. Hard-Tooling Cutoff** | Run side-by-side economics between **standalone laser cutting** (plus secondary forming if needed) and **hard stamping dies** on equal delivered condition | Comparing a flat laser blank unit rate directly against a formed production stamping quote | [Laser Cutting vs. Metal Stamping](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/laser-cutting-vs-stamping/) |
| **Burr & Edge Acceptance Zones** | Replace blanket "no burr" notes with zone-specific criteria: **rollover side vs. burr side direction**, sharp-edge breaking on handling edges, and critical hole/slot clearances | Over-deburring non-critical edges or missing wire/seal-damage burrs in assembly zones | [Stamped Part Burr and Edge Requirements](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/stamped-parts-burr-edge-requirements/) |
| **Stainless Springback & Datum Control** | Specify stainless alloy/condition, rolled-grain relationship to bend line, free-state vs. restrained inspection fixture, and critical angle datums | Assuming carbon-steel bend behavior on cold-worked stainless grades without angular compensation | [Stainless Steel Stamping Springback Guide](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/stainless-steel-stamping-springback/) |
| **Surface Finish & Exposure Zones** | Distinguish visible/cosmetic zones from hidden functional faces, coil film protection, passivation/cleaning requirements, and corrosion-exposure conditions | Leaving surface protection or post-stamp degreasing excluded until first sample inspection | [Stainless Steel Stamping Finish & Corrosion](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/stainless-steel-stamping-surface-finish/) |
| **FAI & Production Release Gate** | Agree pre-production **First Article Inspection (FAI)** sample quantity, critical formed/edge check items, material record scope, and written change-trigger rules | Releasing batch stamping solely on raw hole-diameter checks while bent profiles or datums drift | [First Article Inspection for Metal Stampings](https://beizhoumetal.com/guides/metal-stamping-first-article-inspection/) |

## 2. Quick Rule: Separate Tooling Lifecycle from Recurring Piece Price

Ask each quoting party to keep **tooling build**, **initial tryout/FAI**, **maintenance & storage**, and **recurring batch piece price across quantity breaks** in separate rows. When design revisions remain active during prototyping or initial bridge quantities, evaluate whether [metal stamping coordination](https://beizhoumetal.com/services/metal-stamping/) or blanking plus forming best fits the revision schedule.

## 3. Copy-Ready Non-Confidential Stamping Tooling & Burr RFQ Brief

For a first inquiry, describe the part and share the details you know. You can leave the other fields blank and agree the quote scope later.

```text
Process Route: Metal stamping (specify if bridge laser-blank + forming option should also be evaluated)
Part Summary: [BRACKET / COVER / CLIP / ENCLOSURE SHELL — non-confidential functional outline]
Drawing or model, if known: [REVISION AND UNITS]. Which file defines the finished stamping? [GOVERNING FILE OR TO BE AGREED]
Material Grade, Condition & Thickness: [E.G. 304 / 316L / 5052 / CARBON STEEL, TEMPER/ANNEALED STATE, NOMINAL THICKNESS]
Volume Profile: Tooling Tryout [QTY] | Initial Release [QTY] | Annual Planning Estimate [QTY]
Proposed Die Scope to Price: [Progressive / Transfer / Stage tooling — itemize design, build, tryout & ownership/storage terms]
Critical Forming & Springback Notes: [KEY BEND ANGLES / FLANGES / RESTRAINED VS. FREE-STATE FIXTURE ASSUMPTION]
Burr & Edge Acceptance Zones: [CRITICAL SEALING/HARNESS EDGES, PREFERRED BURR ORIENTATION, EDGE-BREAK REQUIREMENT]
Surface Finish & Protection: [AS-STAMPED / DEGREASED / PASSIVATED / BRUSHED / PROTECTIVE FILM ON COSMETIC ZONES]
FAI & Quality Documentation: [FAI SAMPLE COUNT, CRITICAL DATUM CHECKLIST, MATERIAL RECORD EXPECTATION]
Destination & Timing: [COUNTRY / INCOTERMS / TARGET TOOLING & SAMPLE WINDOW]

Please quote tooling design/build, tryout, and recurring unit pricing separately, and state tooling ownership, maintenance, and excluded secondary steps before quoting.
Non-confidential summary only; please confirm confidentiality terms and transfer method before drawings are sent.
```
