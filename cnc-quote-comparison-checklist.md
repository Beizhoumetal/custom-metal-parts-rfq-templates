# Custom CNC Machining Quote Comparison Worksheet

A practical, non-confidential buyer worksheet for comparing custom CNC machining quotations side by side across revision precedence, finishing, masking, inspection evidence, and delivery scope before issuing a purchase order.

> **Start non-confidential:** Share a non-confidential summary first (part family, material alloy/grade, quantity tiers, surface treatment, and destination country). Agree NDA terms, named manufacturing partner recipients, and an appropriate file-transfer channel before sending proprietary CAD models or 2D drawings.

## 1. Six-Row Comparable Quotation Matrix

Put every prospective bidder into the same six rows—record `"excluded"` or `"open question"` explicitly rather than treating silence as included:

| Comparison Row | Confirm Across Every Bidder | Common Hidden Gap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Drawing & Model Baseline** | Governing drawing revision, units (`mm` / `inch`), and precedence if 2D PDF and 3D STEP differ | Quoting `"latest drawing"` without naming the revision |
| **2. Material & Condition** | Exact alloy/grade, temper or heat-treat condition, stock form, and required material record | Generic labels such as `"aluminum"` or `"stainless steel"` with unapproved substitutions |
| **3. Machining & Secondary Scope** | Deburring, edge-break, thread chasing, hardware insertion, and heat treatment | Assuming secondary operations are included in baseline milling/turning |
| **4. Surface Finish & Masking** | Coating/anodizing spec, cosmetic faces, masking zones, and pre- vs. post-finish inspection of tight bores/threads | Finish build-up shifting critical fit dimensions after inspection |
| **5. Inspection & Sample Evidence** | Critical-to-function features, measurement method (hand gauge vs. CMM), and first-sample vs. batch report format | Requesting `"CMM report"` without naming datums, tolerances, or reporting scope |
| **6. One-Time & Commercial Terms** | Separate setup/fixture charges, unit-price quantity tiers, protective export packing, delivery point, and Incoterms® rule | Comparing a bare blank price against a finished, packed, and inspected offer |

## 2. Clean Total-Cost Comparison Check

Separate one-time preparation charges from recurring per-part economics:

```text
Comparable project scope = agreed one-time setup or fixture charges
                         + (unit price x quoted accepted quantity)
                         + separately quoted finishing, inspection, packing, and delivery costs
```

*Treat any unquoted line item as **"unknown / to be confirmed"** rather than zero, and keep prototype or annual forecast figures separate from committed purchase quantities.*

## 3. Pre-Finish & Tolerance Alignment Checks

- Reserve tight geometric or dimensional limits for functional interfaces (bearing bores, alignment datums, sealing faces) rather than applying a blanket general tolerance everywhere.
- On coated or anodized aluminum parts, explicitly note on the drawing whether critical bores, shafts, and threads are measured before or after surface treatment and whether masking is required.
- Verify first-article sample acceptance criteria and repeat-order change-notification triggers (revision updates, subcontractor changes, or alloy batch changes) in writing.

## 4. Copy-Ready Non-Confidential CNC Quote Alignment Snippet

For a first inquiry, describe the part and share the details you know. You can leave the other fields blank and agree the quote scope later.

```text
Process: CNC machining (milling / turning)
Part summary: [NON-CONFIDENTIAL PART TYPE / FUNCTION]
Drawing or model, if known: [REVISION AND UNITS]. If the drawing and model differ, use [AGREED DOCUMENT OR TO BE CONFIRMED].
Material & condition: [EXACT ALLOY / TEMPER OR GRADE]
Quantities: [PROTOTYPE QTY / INITIAL BATCH / REPEAT ESTIMATE]
Critical tolerances & finish: [KEY BORES / DATUMS / THREADS + ANODIZING / COATING + MASKING OR POST-FINISH ACCEPTANCE]
Inspection scope: [FIRST-SAMPLE REPORT SCOPE VS. PRODUCTION LOT CHECKS]
Destination & timing: [COUNTRY / TARGET DELIVERY WINDOW / INCOTERMS]

Please quote one-time setup or fixture charges separately from unit prices, list all included finishing, inspection, and protective packing, and flag any exclusions or proposed substitutions before we compare offers.
Non-confidential outline only; confirm confidentiality and transfer channel before drawings are sent.
```
