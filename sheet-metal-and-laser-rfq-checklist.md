# Sheet Metal Fabrication & Standalone Laser Cutting RFQ Checklist

Use this non-confidential checklist before issuing an RFQ so every bidder separates **standalone flat-blank laser cutting** from **formed, hardware-inserted, welded, and coated sheet metal assemblies**. Comparing those two scopes on the same unit-price spreadsheet leads to hidden secondary-operation add-ons after award.

> **Start Non-Confidentially:** Share a non-confidential summary first (process route, material grade and thickness, prototype vs. repeat lot size, finishing expectation, and destination country). Confirm NDA coverage, named manufacturing partner recipients, and controlled transfer arrangements before transmitting proprietary 2D drawings or 3D CAD models.

## 2. Quick Comparison Rule: Normalize Setup Charges Separately

Keep one-time programming, laser nesting setup, press-brake tooling checks, and finishing fixture fees separate from recurring per-piece rates at each quantity break. When a quote skips deburring, hardware installation, or protective interleaving, treat that line as **"to be confirmed"** rather than zero cost.

## 3. Copy-Ready Non-Confidential Sheet Metal / Laser RFQ Brief

For a first inquiry, describe the part and share the details you know. You can leave the other fields blank and agree the quote scope later.

```text
Process Route: [Standalone Laser Cutting ONLY / Formed Sheet Metal Fabrication]
Part Description: [BRACKET / ENCLOSURE PANEL / CHASSIS COVER / FLAT BLANK]
File basis, if known: [REVISION AND UNITS]. For the flat profile or finished part, specify which file controls: [FILE AND SCOPE OR TO BE AGREED].
Material & Thickness: [ALLOY / GRADE / CONDITION] — [NOMINAL THICKNESS]
Quantities: Prototype/Sample [QTY] | Initial Lot [QTY] | Annual Estimate [QTY]
Operations Included: [Laser cut only / Bending (bends) / Tapping / Hardware insertion / Welding / Grinding]
Surface Finish & Masking: [As-cut / Powder coat / Plating / Brushed / Critical cosmetic faces & masked areas]
Inspection Scope: [First-sample dimensional report on key datums / Material record / Edge-break check]
Destination & Target Window: [COUNTRY / PORT OR INCOTERMS / TARGET DISPATCH WINDOW]

Please itemize one-time programming/tooling setup charges separately from recurring piece prices, and list all excluded secondary steps or minimum coating lot charges before quoting.
Non-confidential summary only; confirm confidential file-transfer arrangements prior to drawing release.
```
